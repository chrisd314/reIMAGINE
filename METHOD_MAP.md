# Method map

Each method is listed with its purpose, the exact course location it relies on, its assumptions, where it is implemented, and how it is checked. All course pages were read in full on 2026-10-29 (course site, CC BY-SA 4.0). Base URL: `https://sunelehmann.com/socialgraphs2026-web/`.

The **Status** column separates three things:

- **Taught**: the method appears on the cited course page.
- **Taught, applied**: a taught method used here on a new object.
- **Supporting**: a calculation that is not course material.
